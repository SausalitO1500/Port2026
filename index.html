What guides my work

/01 Find the direction

I turn uncertainty into a clear product direction

I look at the problem, the evidence behind it, the outcome we need, and where the greatest opportunity lies. Then I turn that complexity into a clear plan for what matters most and what should happen next.

Skills I bring

  1. /01 Problem framing
  2. /02 Evidence synthesis
  3. /03 Product judgement
  4. /04 Opportunity framing
  5. /05 Prioritization

/02 Shape the product

I design products that evolve as people’s needs change

I design for the need in front of us without trapping the product there. I consider how behaviors, expectations, and the market are changing so the product can grow while staying useful to the people it serves.

Skills I bring

  1. /01 Future-state thinking
  2. /02 Behavioral insight
  3. /03 Experience strategy
  4. /04 Scalable design
  5. /05 Product evolution

/03 Move decisions forward

I make the case with work that builds conviction

I turn research, iteration, and design decisions into work that makes the opportunity tangible. A resolved product experience and clear rationale help leaders see the value, understand the impact, and make confident investment decisions.

Skills I bring

  1. /01 Design rationale
  2. /02 Concept storytelling
  3. /03 Experience definition
  4. /04 Stakeholder influence
  5. /05 Impact framing

I turn complexity into products that make life work better.

Over the past 10 years, I’ve designed products, services, and strategies across financial services, healthcare, entertainment, and more recently enterprise AI.

Today, I work across payments at Visa, bringing product design, research, and strategy together to shape complex product challenges. Before that, I helped define future mobile banking experiences at Citi. My agency years built the foundation for how I still work today: adapt quickly, learn fast, and stay curious in unfamiliar problem spaces.

What comes next is about helping define a new generation of AI-native products that remain deeply human-led. I want to bring together what I’ve learned about people, products, creativity, and rigor to build technology that expands opportunity, improves everyday life, and makes the benefits of AI useful to more people.
