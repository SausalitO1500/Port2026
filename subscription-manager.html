Making recurring spending easier to see, understand, and manage

Subscriptions are easy to lose track of until the charge appears. Customers needed a clearer way to see what they were paying for, understand what was coming next, and take action when something no longer worked for them.

See it. Manage it. Cancel it.

Role

Design Lead

Client

PNC

Challenge

PNC wanted to help customers get a clearer picture of recurring expenses, but the existing experience was hard to find, mostly informational, and did little to help people take action.

My Contribution

I lead the experience from an open brief to three focused, tested journeys: view, manage, and cancel subscriptions, turning a broad product idea into a clear MVP ready to move toward build.

Impact

The final direction defined and tested the MVP, giving the bank clear user evidence and a practical path toward build and investment.

The experience needed to feel simple

Customers wanted the important things to be obvious without having to read too much or search for answers.
